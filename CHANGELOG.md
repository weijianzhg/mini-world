# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-12

First public release of Mini World.

- Explore a 3D globe with forests, deserts, oceans and snow.
- Control four people and 24 animals using mouse, keyboard or touch.
- Visit furnished houses, use beds and tables, drink water and climb stairs.
- Guide animals to their own resting homes within their home region.
- Collect six kinds of shapes, zoom, rotate, hop and swim.
- Add a standalone static preview, installation guide, MIT license and automated checks.
- Fix mouse dragging after clicking an occupied animal home.
